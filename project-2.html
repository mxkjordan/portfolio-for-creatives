LexiLoop

An app interface targeted towards people that are dyslexic, this interface finds all the resources that you need for you to have a better understanding and supports you as well!

This is a project that was created as a resource for people that have dyslexia and need a resource if they don't have all of the access to their resources.

This was done by a group of students that were dyslexic, so all agreed that it was something where we can help. Due to the fact that there are school that specalize for students with dyslexia are super expensive and some people might not have the money to find those resources.
